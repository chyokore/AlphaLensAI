### 4. Risk Analysis
- **Expectancy Risk:** Formula: `(Win% × Avg Win) – (Loss% × Avg Loss)`. Your current average PnL is negative, meaning expectancy is <0. Every trade, on average, costs capital.
- **Asymmetric Loss Exposure:** A 56.25% win rate with negative PnL implies your average loss is likely 1.5x–2x larger than your average win. This makes the portfolio highly vulnerable to drawdowns during losing streaks.
- **Behavioral/Confidence Risk:** Trading with high confidence on negative-expectancy setups increases position size exposure and emotional attachment to losing trades, raising the risk of revenge trading or stop-loss overrides.
- **Statistical Risk:** With only 16 trades, variance can easily mask a real edge or exaggerate a flawed one. Medium risk + negative expectancy = compounding capital erosion over time.

### 5. Recommended Actions
1. **Enforce Positive Expectancy Rules:**
   - Calculate your actual Average Win vs. Average Loss in R-multiples or %.
   - Target a minimum reward-to-risk ratio of **1:1.5** (ideally 1:2). Cut losses quickly; let winners run with trailing stops or structured take-profit levels.

2. **Recalibrate Confidence Scoring:**
   - Track confidence vs. realized PnL per trade. Only allocate full position size when historical data shows ≥60% win rate *and* positive expectancy for that confidence tier.
   - Downgrade or skip setups where confidence >70% but expectancy is unproven.

3. **Increase Sample Size & Track Rigorously:**
   - Aim for **50–100 trades** under identical rules before evaluating edge.
   - Journal: Entry rationale, confidence, stop/target, outcome, slippage, emotional state. Use this to isolate which setups drive negative PnL.

4. **Implement Strict Risk Controls:**
   - Cap risk per trade at **1–2% of account equity**.
   - Use daily/weekly loss limits (e.g., -3% daily halt) to prevent drawdown compounding.
   - Review position sizing: Medium risk is acceptable only if expectancy turns positive.

5. **Strategy Refinement & Validation:**
   - Backtest or forward-test modifications in a simulated environment until expectancy is consistently >0.
   - Consider focusing on fewer, higher-probability setups rather than trading volume.
   - If win rate remains >55% but PnL stays negative, the issue is purely trade management (exits, scaling, stops). Fix exits first.
